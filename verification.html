What we measure, and what we found.

A formal statement can compile and still say the wrong thing. We check whether it says what the mathematics says.

Benchmark audits

1,347 statements across miniF2F, miniF2F v2 and ProofNet#, every flag verified by hand. 76 confirmed defects, all three filings public: miniF2F, v2, ProofNet#.

Cross-system audits

Four open autoformalizers, same 488 problems, their own prompts. Failure rates ran from 21.7% to 55.5%. A compile check catches none of it.

Specification gaming

59 pairs satisfy the checker and two frontier judges yet fail human review. Three failure classes now have mechanical detectors.
